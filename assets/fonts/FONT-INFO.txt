Packaging changes, 15 October 2026:
This website includes four faces from the full font family. Their
OpenType bytes are unmodified and are embedded as base64 data URLs
in latin-modern.css for use both offline and on static web hosting.
The CSS wrapper and selection of four faces are specific to this website.
No glyphs, font names, metrics, or internal font metadata were changed.
The original font authors do not maintain this website's packaging.

The complete, unmodified Latin Modern distribution is available from:
http://www.gust.org.pl/projects/e-foundry/latin-modern/
https://ctan.org/pkg/lm

The original README, full distribution manifest, GUST Font License,
and LPPL 1.3c text are included in this folder. The original manifest
describes the complete upstream distribution, not just these four faces.
